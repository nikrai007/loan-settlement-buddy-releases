# Release notes

Newest first. Versions follow `vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.

## v1.0.0 — 22 November 2026

The first full version of Loan Settlement Buddy: a complete website plus a private portal for every customer.
It replaces the one-page site.

**For visitors**
- A full website: services, bank guides, loan categories, city pages, calculators, FAQ and legal pages.
- A two-step quick enquiry form, and a free 10-minute assessment that you can pause and resume.
- The website in 10 Indian languages — हिन्दी, मराठी, বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, ગુજરાતી, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ଓଡ଼ିଆ — switched
  with one tap from the top of the page, on any phone or computer. A small "English" button brings you back at any time.
  The English version of our legal pages is the binding one.
- "Download App": add Loan Settlement Buddy to your phone's home screen or your computer like an app.
- Paid phone and video consultations with secure payment confirmation.

**For customers**
- Your own case room: every stage of your settlement, documents, offers, messages, letters and receipts in one place.
- An evidence locker for recovery-agent harassment and a credit score tracker.
- If you come back with another loan, it opens as a new case under the same account.
- Sign in with mobile OTP, email OTP or password; automatic sign-out on shared devices.

**Privacy and security**
- Nothing reaches a lender without your written consent.
- Your contact details are hidden from staff by default; every reveal is recorded.
- Short links and QR codes never contain your details.
